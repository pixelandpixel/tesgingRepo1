Physics NotesLearn
Forces during flight
• Gravity: Fg = m g
• Drag: Fd = 0.5 ρ Cd A v² (opposite velocity)
• Magnus: sideways/lift from spin: Fm ∝ v × ω
• Swing (simplified): lateral acceleration depends on speed + swing strength

Bounce model
• Normal restitution (hardness): controls bounce height
• Tangential friction: controls skid/hold
• Seam bite: adds extra sideways deflection on impact
